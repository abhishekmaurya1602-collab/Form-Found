Accessibility at Form & Found.

Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. We want everyone to be able to discover the objects, meet their makers, and follow a new idea.

Using a keyboard

  • Press Tab to move between links and controls, and Shift + Tab to move back. A visible outline shows your position.
  • The first link skips directly to the gallery. Use Enter to open links and Enter or Space to activate buttons.
  • Press Control + K, or Command + K on a Mac, to focus search when a dialog is closed.
  • Object stories and maker profiles open in dialogs. Press Escape or use the close button to return to the gallery. The exploration Back button returns to your previous story.

Reading and exploring

  • Text and control colors use a higher-contrast palette. Controls have visible labels or accessible names.
  • The layout adapts to narrow screens and enlarged text. Your device’s reduced-motion and high-contrast settings are respected.
  • More stories load when you choose the “More good things” button. Focus moves to the new content, and the change is announced to screen readers.
  • Saving, following, search results, and copy-link feedback are announced without moving you away from the action. Messages do not cover the controls.
  • External archive links identify when they open a new tab. Photographs have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.

Assessment status

The implementation was reviewed on 15 October 2026. Automated checks cover page structure, accessible names, dialog content, application focus behavior, and the contrast of the authored color palette.

A complete manual assessment with real browsers and assistive technology is still outstanding. Native dialog behavior, screen-reader announcements, enlarged text, text-spacing changes, and reflow need that final review. We are not claiming independently verified WCAG conformance.

Report an accessibility problem

If something prevents you from using the site, report it in the project’s GitHub issue tracker. Include the page, what you were trying to do, and your browser or assistive technology. A GitHub account is required to submit an issue.
